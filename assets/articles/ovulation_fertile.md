# What is Ovulation & The Fertile Window?

Ovulation occurs once per cycle when a mature ovarian follicle releases an ovum (egg). Understanding this timing helps whether you are planning a pregnancy or tracking your natural rhythms.

## The Fertile Window Explained
While an egg is only viable for approximately 12–24 hours after release, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days under optimal conditions.
- **Window Length:** ~6 days (5 days before ovulation + the day of ovulation).
- **Peak Chance:** The 2 days leading up to ovulation and the day itself.

## How to Recognize Your Fertile Days
1. **Cervical Fluid:** Shifts to a clear, slippery, stretchy texture resembling raw egg whites.
2. **Basal Body Temperature (BBT):** Rises by 0.3°C–0.5°C *after* ovulation occurs due to progesterone.
3. **Mild Mittelschmerz:** Some women experience a slight twinge or cramp on one side of the lower abdomen.
